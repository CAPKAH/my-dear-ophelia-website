Welcome to the official My Dear Ophelia website!!

My Dear Ophelia is a virtual pet game where you can take care of your new best friend, Ophelia!

Feed her, give her something to drink, choose her hairstyle, dress her with cute clothes, decorate her with accessories and make sure she is always happy! Play fun mini-games to earn coins, then use them to buy food, drinks, outfits and many other lovely surprises.

There are always new things to discover, so don't forget to visit again for future updates!
