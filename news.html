Basic Needs System

Taking care of Ophelia is more than just playing games and dressing her up! We've added four basic needs that you'll have to keep an eye on: Energy, Hunger, Hygiene, and Health.

Every interaction with Ophelia can affect her needs, and they can also affect one another. Playing too much can make her tired, while neglecting her hygiene can eventually affect her health. Keep an eye on Ophelia and make sure she has everything she needs to stay happy and healthy!

Ophelia's Wardrobe

Give Ophelia a style that's all her own! Her wardrobe includes all kinds of cosmetics, hairstyles, shirts, dresses, and plenty of other accessories to choose from. Mix and match different pieces to create the perfect look for her!

With so many combinations available, you can change Ophelia's appearance whenever you want. Try different styles, experiment with new outfits, and find the ones that suit her best.

After all, Ophelia is counting on you to decide what she should wear.
